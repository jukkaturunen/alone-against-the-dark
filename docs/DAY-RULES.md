# Multi-day play in Arkham

Page references use printed numbering.

## Calendar, food, and sleep

- Account for every hour on the 1931 Calendar and record the game date and time in the journal. The calendar distinguishes noon and midnight. Local travel takes one hour, as does each search topic or interview unless an entry specifies otherwise. Entry 13 starts on 1 September and sends Grunewald to entry 102 upon waking on 2 September. The exact opening hours in [OPENING.md](OPENING.md) are adaptation choices. (Adventure pp. 7–8, 48–49, entry 13 pp. 11–12.)
- Allow two one-hour meals each day, separated by at least six hours. A day may defer one or both meals, but both current meals and any deferred meals are due the following day. When unable to eat, spend the meal periods resting for the same duration and lose 1 HP for each day of fasting. Extra meals cannot cover future days. (Adventure p. 7.)
- Allow eight continuous hours of sleep each day. Any shortfall must extend the following night's continuous block. Extra sleep cannot count toward future nights. The book uses nights for sleep debt and days for meal debt. Natural recovery is 1 HP per day and 1 MP per hour up to normal maximum. (Adventure p. 7.)
- Grunewald can rest and have a meal at home through 8 September. Bee's Diner serves meals 6 AM–5 PM Monday–Friday, combining the stated hours and weekday schedule. Meal access depends on the location. (Adventure p. 7, entry 102 p. 25, entry 593 p. 90, Handouts p. 3.)

## Daily attempts and dated entries

- Rolls for obtaining information or interviews at a location are available once daily unless the entry says otherwise. Harding House room entry 150 explicitly allows one attempt per listed skill per day at one roll per hour. University library entry 145 allows one attempt each hour. The opening applies the daily limit per newspaper approach and preserves discoveries across visits. (Adventure p. 8, entry 150 p. 31, entry 145 p. 30, entry 60 p. 19.)
- From 9 September, home entry 102 routes to 147 and university entry 82 routes Fen and the Dean to 143 and 197 instead of 122 and 100. From 1 October, newspaper entry 60 adds entry 172. The introduction establishes a race against time without giving a single global deadline. These dates govern entry branches. (Adventure pp. 3, 7, entry 102 p. 25, entry 82 p. 22, entry 60 p. 19.)

## Implementation decisions still open

- The book does not assign an overnight sleep block to a specific calendar date or specify when to book HP changes. The opening's 8 PM rest availability and 11 PM checkpoint are adaptation choices. Legacy saves record a sleep total, meal start timestamps, and attempt counts. Preserve recorded outcomes and distinguish a completed overnight rest from a late-night checkpoint when continuing a save.
- The source does not define whether the six-hour interval is measured from meal start or meal end, whether it applies between dates, or how it applies to make-up meals. The opening measures start to start. It also leaves partial fasting, repeated missed debts, and the timing of substitute rest unspecified.
- Entry 60's “once” can describe a roll within an hour or each listed approach. Retaining the opening's daily interpretation is a continuity choice. Use the current calendar date for day-specific rules, keep historical rolls and clues, and avoid treating a change of date as a reset of investigator skills.
